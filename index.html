SentinelX: Open-source SOC platform

Security operations for teams without a 24/7 SOC. SentinelX turns thousands of raw security events into a handful of explainable incidents, each with a risk score you can defend.

  1. Wazuh · Suricata · Zeek

  3. Sigma + ML
  4. Correlate
  5. ATT&CK + risk

05 Accessibility · KARE × Purdue

Vision Aid (formerly Magnifier)

An assistive tool for visually impaired users, built by a joint team of students from Kalasalingam University and Purdue University. It reads text from documents aloud and adapts the display for low vision and color blindness.

  1. Upload PDF
  2. Render pages
  3. Tesseract OCR
  4. Review text
  5. Text-to-speech
  • Renders each PDF page as an image with PyMuPDF and Pillow, then extracts the text with Tesseract OCR, so scanned documents work too.
  • Converts the text to speech in English, Spanish, French, German or Hindi with gTTS, for playback in the app or download.
  • 16 display filters, including high-contrast pairs (yellow on blue, yellow on black) and protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia modes. Built by a cross-university team of five.
  • Python
  • Streamlit
  • PyMuPDF
  • Pillow
  • Tesseract OCR
  • gTTS
06 Health · Responsible AI

MindLoop: Wellbeing companion

A privacy-first, culturally adaptive companion for students and early-career professionals in India: check in, get one small evidence-informed action, and learn which actions actually help.

  1. Check in
  2. Safety layer
  3. Pick action
  4. Did it help?
  5. Patterns
  • A deterministic safety classifier runs before any model call. High-risk messages get reviewed templates and verified human resources, never generated text.
  • Interventions are retrieved from 23 structured, versioned entries; the LLM only phrases them. Personal patterns are explainable statistics, not black-box ML.
  • Content encrypted at rest, audio deleted after transcription; a tap check-in costs zero LLM calls.
